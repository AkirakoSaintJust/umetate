## Fixes included in this baseline

The clean-clone verification surfaced two latent target-build defects, both fixed
and re-verified before tagging:

- `firmware/tools/build.py`: the `.dsp_text` calls/branches check compared the
  branch's relative offset to absolute section bounds (false positives masked a
  real XIP call).
- `firmware/hal/fm1_math.h`: the saturating-math helpers were `static inline` but
  the pi32v2 clang emitted them as XIP functions, so `.dsp_text` called into
  flash. They are now `always_inline`.
