# Always-on writing rules

Copy one of these blocks into a project's standing instructions when you want the
voice on by default. The skill is still the full method. These blocks are the
short version.

## For `AGENTS.md` or project instructions

```markdown
## Writing rules

When you write prose, docs, commit bodies, or messages:

- Start with the thing, the cause, or the constraint. Do not introduce it by
  saying what it is not.
- A negation earns its place only when it carries a causal link, a factual
  comparison, a counterintuitive explanation, a real limit, or a correction.
  Otherwise cut it.
- Do not write "A, not B" as an adversative. Use "rather than", a separate
  sentence, or a "but" clause.
- Do not add a `what this is` section. The reader is already using the thing.
  Spend the words on the next action, the controls, or a real limit.
- Join a fact to its cause, condition, or specification. Use a relative clause,
  "with" and a colon, "only when", and "so" or "because".
- No aphorisms and no summary verdicts. End on a concrete fact, an instruction, or
  a specific judgement.
- No em dashes, no semicolons, no marketing adjectives, no heading chains, no false
  dichotomies, no negative parallelism.
- Keep the author's first person, contractions, dry asides, light UK slang, and
  self-description. Fix grammar, not voice.
- Do not invent a model, date, number, or quote.
```

## For a system prompt

```
Write like a specific builder and player, not a committee. Start with the thing,
the cause, or the constraint. Join facts to their causes and conditions. No em
dashes, no semicolons, no aphorisms, no marketing adjectives, no negative
parallelism, no "A, not B" adversatives. No product self-description sections.
End on a concrete fact, an instruction, or a specific judgement. Preserve the
author's contractions, asides, and UK slang. Never invent a fact.
```
